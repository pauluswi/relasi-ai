For this synthetic demonstration, capture the card transaction date, amount, merchant descriptor, and masked card reference. Never request the full card number, PIN, password, or one-time code.

Confirm whether the customer recognizes the merchant and whether the card is still in their possession. Record the customer's answers and the preferred contact channel in the interaction history.

Create a CARD_ISSUE or TRANSACTION_DISPUTE case according to the reported issue. Keep the case advisory and route it to an authorized service officer for review; do not reverse or execute a payment from the assistant.

This demonstration procedure is synthetic and does not represent a real bank's card-dispute policy.
