Use the customer's stated needs and current product disclosures as evidence; a product name, segment, balance, or risk indicator alone is not enough to determine suitability.

For a savings account or debit card, ask whether access, transaction limits, or service support meet the customer's needs. For a credit card, review the customer's question and explain current approved fees and payment terms.
