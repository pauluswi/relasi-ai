For this synthetic demonstration, confirm the mobile application version, device type, and the exact error message. Do not request a password, PIN, or one-time code.

Ask the customer to check their network connection and device date and time, then retry once. If access still fails, record the error and channel in an interaction and create a MOBILE_BANKING_ISSUE case.

An authorized service officer may route the case to digital banking support. The assistant must not reset credentials or change account security settings.

This demonstration procedure is synthetic and does not represent a real bank's mobile banking policy.
